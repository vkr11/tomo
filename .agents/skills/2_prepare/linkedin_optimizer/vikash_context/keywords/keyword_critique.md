# Keyword Critique — Grounded Against Real JDs

I pulled actual job descriptions from **Anthropic** (Greenhouse board), **Google** (careers.google.com), **OpenAI** (openai.com/careers), and **Nvidia** (nvidia.com) for Director/Principal/GPM AI PM roles. Here's an honest critique of the keyword list I gave you earlier.

---

## ❌ What I Got Wrong (Over-Indexed)

| Keyword I Recommended | Problem |
|---|---|
| **P&L Ownership / Revenue Growth** | Almost none of these AI lab JDs mention P&L. This is a keyword for consumer/marketplace companies (Uber, DoorDash, Intuit) — not for OpenAI/Anthropic/Google AI. These labs care about **model capability improvement and user adoption**, not revenue attribution. Over-indexing on P&L could actually signal "enterprise PM" rather than "frontier AI PM." |
| **ARR/MRR scaling** | Same issue. Completely absent from research-heavy AI PM JDs. Relevant only if you're targeting Intuit or a B2B SaaS company. |
| **Chain of Thought (CoT) Reasoning** | Too implementation-specific. JDs mention it as part of "model capabilities" but never as a standalone keyword. Using it in your headline would read as an ML engineer, not a PM. Keep it in experience bullet points only. |
| **DPO** | Too niche. Only appears in ML research postings, never in PM JDs. Remove. |
| **Latency & Inference Optimization** | This shows up in Nvidia's JDs but not in PM JDs at Anthropic/OpenAI/Google. It's an engineering concern. Mentioning it in passing (e.g., "shipped inference improvements") is fine, but it shouldn't be a primary keyword. |

## ⚠️ What's Correct But Needs Company-Specific Tuning

The original list treated all 13 target companies as one bucket. They're not. Here's how keywords cluster by company archetype:

### Tier 1: Frontier AI Labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind)
**What they actually search for:**
- Model Alignment, Model Behavior, Steerability
- Translate Research into Product (exact phrase)
- First-Principles Thinking
- 0-to-1 in ambiguous environments
- Python/SQL proficiency
- Founder DNA / shipped fast in chaos
- Safety, Red-Teaming, Adversarial Thinking
- Agentic Systems

**What they do NOT care about:**
- P&L, ARR, Revenue Growth, GTM Strategy

### Tier 2: AI Platform Companies (Google Cloud, Nvidia, Microsoft AI)
**What they actually search for:**
- Developer Tools, SDKs, APIs, Developer Experience
- GPU Architecture, Distributed Systems, Inference
- Open-Source Ecosystem (PyTorch, Nemo)
- Research-to-Production pipeline
- Hardware/Software Co-Design (Nvidia specifically)
- Enterprise AI Deployment

**What they do NOT care about:**
- Consumer metrics (DAU/MAU), Model Alignment details

### Tier 3: Consumer/Marketplace (Uber, DoorDash, Netflix, Coupang, Apple)
**What they actually search for:**
- P&L Ownership, Revenue Growth, Unit Economics
- Operational Excellence, Marketplace Dynamics
- A/B Testing, Experimentation Frameworks
- Growth Strategy, Retention, Churn
- Craft and Design Excellence (Apple)

**What they do NOT care about:**
- LLM internals, Model Alignment, RLHF

### Tier 4: Enterprise Tech (Intuit, Microsoft, Meta)
**What they actually search for:**
- GTM Strategy, Enterprise Sales Motion
- Platform Thinking, Ecosystem Strategy
- Customer-Driven Innovation (Intuit's "Design for Delight")
- Cross-Functional Leadership at Scale
- Data-Driven Decision Making
