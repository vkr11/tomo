### Interview Story Development - Meta Ads Fairness Project

- Working on behavioral interview story about leading ads fairness initiative at Meta
- Managed $3B+ revenue across housing, employment, credit (HEC) verticals
- Need crisp opening line followed by context, not philosophical approach

### Story Structure Refinement

- Opening approach:
  - Start with impact: “As PM, led initiative when $3B revenue under regulatory threat”
  - Three challenge angles: regulatory, user experience, team morale
  - Avoid fairness terminology - use “responsibility” instead
- Context setting:
  1. External regulatory pressure (DOJ scrutiny)
  2. Meta’s broader market challenges (Apple signal loss, revenue pressure)
  3. Internal team morale issues

### Technical Solution Framework

- Problem identification:
  - Models reflected societal bias, needed debiasing beyond society norms
  - HEC verticals impact core life opportunities (housing, jobs, credit)
  - Legacy system had hundreds of models with discriminatory patterns
- Key intervention: Forked entire ad system
  - Rebuilt targeting, ranking, delivery systems specifically for HEC
  - Converted 400+ models to 15 focused models
  - Isolated regulatory risk from broader ad ecosystem

### Business Impact & Validation

- Secured regulatory approval:
  - Signed agreement with HUD (Housing & Urban Development)
  - DOJ partnership established
  - Created perception of fairness vs absolute fairness
- Revenue protection & growth:
  - Performance initially declined but top-of-funnel increased
  - Advertisers like Citibank brought $100M+ spend after regulatory clearance
  - 20% growth achieved through feature parity restoration
