## 🅰️ Sourcing

The single highest-conversion channel at IC7/D1 is a warm intro from a senior leader who can vouch for your judgment.

| Tactic | How |
|--------|-----|
| **"The Check-In"** | Quarterly coffee with 10-15 former peers who are now Directors/VPs. Don't ask for jobs — ask about their challenges. |
| **"The Broker"** | Identify 2-3 natural connectors (ex-Chiefs of Staff, senior EMs, VC operators). Give them your 1-pager. |
| **"The Backchannel"** | When you see a role posted, find someone *inside* that org. Ask: "Is this real? Who's the HM? What's the actual problem?" |
| **"The VC Loop"** | For startup/growth roles: reach out to portfolio partners. They actively match senior talent to their companies. |

**Weak vs Strong:**

| Dimension | ❌ Weak | ✅ Strong |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| **Ask** | "Any openings at your company?" | "I saw [Company] is investing in [X]. I led a similar bet — would love your take on the org." |
| **Timing** | Reach out only when you need something | Maintain relationships consistently; the ask is a natural next step |
| **Specificity** | "Looking for director-level roles" | "Looking for a 0→1 AI product role with a team of 20+, reporting to a VP" |

## 🅱️ Assets & Optics

### Resume

Your resume must answer "Does this person operate at IC7/D1 scope?" in under 30 seconds.

| Dimension | ❌ Weak | ✅ Strong |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| **Scope** | "Led product for the notifications team" | "Owned the $400M notification platform serving 2B+ users across 4 product lines" |
| **Impact** | "Improved user engagement" | "Grew D28 retention 22%, adding $85M incremental ARR" |
| **Leadership** | "Managed 5 engineers" | "Built a 40-person cross-functional org (PM, Eng, DS, Design) across 3 charters" |
| **Strategy** | "Worked on the AI strategy" | "Authored the 3-year AI roadmap, approved by C-suite, resulting in 2 new product lines" |

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Every bullet quantifies scope (ARR, team size, users)
- [ ] At least one 0→1 *and* one 1→100 initiative
- [ ] At least one org-design or hiring bullet
- [ ] Results describe *business outcomes*, not just metrics
- [ ] Max 2 pages. "I" not "we".

### LinkedIn

| Element | ❌ Weak | ✅ Strong |
|---------|--------|----------|
| **Headline** | "Product Manager at Meta" | "Director of Product · AI/ML & Ads · Ex-Meta · Ex-Google" |
| **About** | Generic responsibilities | Specific narrative: philosophy, scale, superpower |
| **Featured** | Empty | A talk, an article, a Substack post |

**Keyword targets:** Product Strategy, Org Design, 0 to 1, Cross-functional Leadership, + your domain (AI/ML, Ads, Enterprise, etc.)

## 🅲 Recruiter Screen

Goal: pass the level check, extract intel, get to the HM fast.

### Your 2-Min Pitch

```text
IDENTITY   (10s)  → "I'm a Director-level PM with 12 years at Meta and Google..."
SUPERPOWER (20s)  → "I specialize in taking ambiguous 0→1 bets and scaling them..."
PROOF      (40s)  → "Most recently, I built [X] from scratch to $Y ARR..."
WHY HERE   (20s)  → "What excites me about [Company] is [specific thing]..."
CLOSE      (10s)  → "I'd love to learn more about the role and the team."
```

### Level Confirmation

Say this early: *"To make sure we're aligned — I'm targeting Director / Principal IC (IC7/D1) roles. Does this position match that scope?"*

### Intel to Extract

| Question | Why |
|----------|-----|
| "Why is this role open? Backfill or net new?" | Net new = vision role. Backfill = execution/fix role. |
| "What is the 12-month mandate?" | Reveals what success looks like. |
| "Who does this report to?" | VP = real scope. Sr. Dir = may be lower than described. |
| "Why have previous candidates not worked out?" | Reveals hidden requirements or cultural landmines. |

### Red Flags

| Signal | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| Recruiter can't articulate the mandate | Role is poorly defined or a fishing expedition |
| "Also manage a team of 3" | This is an IC5/IC6 being sold as IC7 |
| No clarity on reporting structure | Org is in flux |
| "We need someone ASAP" | Desperation hire — could be great or a dumpster fire |

## 🅳 Hiring Manager Call

This is a peer conversation, not a quiz. The HM is calibrating: *"Can I see this person as a strategic peer on my leadership team?"*

### Mindset

| ❌ Interview Posture | ✅ Peer Posture |
|---------------------|----------------|
| "I need to impress them" | "We are evaluating each other" |
| Wait for questions | Drive the conversation with curiosity |
| Recite achievements | Connect your experience to their pain points |

### Superpower → Pain Point Bridge

| Your Superpower | Their Pain | Your Bridge |
|----------------|-----------|-------------|
| Turnaround expert | Product declining | "I've reversed negative trajectories twice — happy to share the playbook." |
| 0→1 visionary | New area, no PM | "I've built 3 products from whitespace to $100M+ ARR." |
| Org builder | Team underperforming | "I've restructured PM orgs of 20+ across multiple charters." |

### Questions to Ask

| Question | What It Reveals |
|----------|----------------|
| "What's the existential risk for this product in 18 months?" | Strategic thinking + whether product is in danger |
| "What does wild success look like in 1 year? 3 years?" | Clarity of vision — if they can't answer, the role is undefined |
| "How are decisions made between Product, Eng, and Design?" | Power dynamics and where PM actually sits |
| "What's the biggest bet you made that didn't work?" | Psychological safety — vulnerability = good sign |

### Closing

**Interested:** *"This is exactly the kind of ambiguous, high-scale problem I'm drawn to. I'd love to move forward."*

**Unsure:** *"I'm impressed by the scope. Can we reconnect in 48 hours after I think through how my background maps?"*

## Antipatterns

| ❌ Antipattern | ✅ Fix |
|---------------|-------|
| Spray-and-pray applications | 10 warm intros > 100 cold apps |
| "Open to opportunities" on LinkedIn | Be specific: "Exploring Director-level AI product roles" |
| Leading with comp in recruiter screens | Lead with scope & problem space curiosity |
| Treating HM calls like interviews | Drive the conversation; ask as many questions as you answer |
| No homework on the company | 30 min on earnings calls, blog posts, org charts |
| Waiting until "ready" to network | Start 6 months before you plan to search |
