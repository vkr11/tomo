# IC7 / D1 Interview Master Guide

This is the central entry point for your IC7/D1 interview preparation. It outlines the overall strategy, logical pairings, and prep execution.

## 🎯 Phase 0: Top of the Funnel (Pipeline Generation)

Before diving into the core framework, you need to generate high-quality inbound interest and pass the initial gating. See the **[Top of the Funnel Guide](./00_top_of_funnel.md)** for targeted strategies on:
*   **Resume & LinkedIn**: Optimizing for IC7/D1 scale, scope, and inbound recruiter search indexing.
*   **Recruiter Screens**: Bypassing low-level standard filters and validating role level/mandate.
*   **Hiring Manager (HM) Calls**: Conducting peer-level consultative discussions and establishing mutual value.

## Interview Strategy

### Company-Specific Lens

| Company | Culture Code | What to Signal |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Google** | Consensus, rigor, data | Show coalition-building, use frameworks |
| **Meta** | Speed, bias to action | Show iteration, quantify everything |
| **Amazon** | Leadership Principles | Map every story to an LP, use "I" not "we" |

---

### The 5 Stories You Must Have

| # | Story Type | Primary Pillar(s) | Secondary Pillar(s) |
|---|------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Turnaround** | Execution (3) | Management (5), Strategy (1) |
| 2 | **0 → 1** | Strategy (1) | Sense (2), Technical (7) |
| 3 | **People Crisis** | Leadership (6) | Management (5) |
| 4 | **Technical Bet** | Technical (7) | Strategy (1), Execution (3) |
| 5 | **Failure** | Leadership (6) | Metrics (4), Domain (8) |

---

### Communication Tactics

| Tactic | How |
|--------|-----|
| **BLUF** | Bottom Line Up Front. Answer first, explain second. |
| **Minto Pyramid** | Conclusion → key points → supporting details |
| **Quantify** | "Improved retention" → "Improved D7 retention 18%" |
| **Pause** | 3-5 sec silence before answering shows thoughtfulness |
| **Close strong** | "Does that answer, or should I go deeper on X?" |

---

### Weekly Prep Cadence

| Week | Focus | Activities |
|------|-------|------------|
| 1-2 | **Strategy (1) + Sense (2)** | Mock "Design X" + "3-year vision for Y" |
| 3-4 | **Execution (3) + Metrics (4)** | "How would you ship...?" + "Debug this metric drop" |
| 5-6 | **Mgmt (5) + Leadership (6)** | Write 5 stories + practice "influence" & "conflict" |
| 7-8 | **Technical (7) + Domain (8)** | Whiteboard systems + deep-dive domain (Ads/AI/etc.) |
| Ongoing | **Full Loops** | 2x/week mocks across all pillars |

### Prep Prioritization

**Table Stakes (Must be strong)**
*   Strategy (1)
*   Execution (3)
*   Leadership & Drive (6)

**Differentiators (Can win the offer)**
*   Sense (2) - for consumer PM roles
*   Domain (8) - for specialist roles (Ads PM, ML PM, etc.)
*   Technical (7) - for infra-heavy products

**Support Pillars (Should be "good enough")**
*   Metrics (4)
*   Management (5)

---

### Day-of Tactics

1. **Energy**: Eat protein, hydrate, light exercise morning-of
2. **Calibrate**: Ask interviewer "What does great look like for this role?"
3. **Clarify**: Always ask 1-2 clarifying questions before diving in
4. **Whiteboard**: Draw while you talk—shows structured thinking
5. **Close**: End with "What questions do you have for me?"
